## Behavior

- Every three simulation seconds, assess own squads, team-observed contacts, public objectives, and finite owned caches. The defensive mode keeps its 90-second preparation period.
- Guard captured objectives and advance in short bounds. Cover selection samples local space and scores protection across a squad-sized area, spacing, travel and firing lines; actual navigation still validates routes.
- Stationary, fit, sufficiently supplied squads can cover a lateral maneuver. A squad being ordered to move or withdraw in the same decision is not counted as stationary support.
- Suppression interrupts advances; depleted, exhausted, badly wounded or demoralized squads pull back or rally. They retain ordinary firing/spotting restrictions and receive no accuracy or damage bonus.
- Low ammunition sends troops to a finite, owned, uncontested cache. Transfers still require physical arrival and stopping. Loading persists through its initial threshold; a newly lost/empty cache invalidates that supply plan. No remote refill, healing or replenished force.
- Commitments limit task switching. Ordinary order changes have a five-second cooldown; rate-limited requests retry instead of falsely committing to unissued orders. Stalled plans are reconsidered after 15 seconds, but arbitrary unreachable geometry is not guaranteed recoverable.
- Versioned plans persist through save/load. Old saves without enemy memory are accepted. Invalid plans and incompatible memory versions are rejected before replacing an active campaign.
- Enemy plan reasons and hidden state are diagnostic data, not player intelligence. Normal map/labels still use spotted or stationary last-known contacts.

The planner is rule-based, **not trained or learning**. It knows the map geometry and public flag ownership, but its observation boundary excludes opposing live positions, health, personal supplies and orders. Individual shooters still need their own sight line.

## Remaining limits / next checks

- Player judgment of believable and enjoyable behavior remains open. These matches do not establish balanced difficulty; the exposed defense is dangerous and the 48-vs-24 offensive is forgiving.
- Local cover search and squad-level morale/withdrawal are not individual panic, surrender, morale contagion, formation tactics, or a full opposing logistics/garrison campaign. Resupply uses existing captured town caches, not new enemy trucks or invisible off-map stock.
- Team contacts are shared promptly. There is no radio/message-delay simulation or predictive knowledge of future events.
- Browser navigation is asynchronous; exact headless deterministic continuation does not promise wall-clock-identical browser replays.
- Large-camp performance remains an outstanding prior limitation; this pass does not re-certify it. No neural study results are added or pooled with older rules.
