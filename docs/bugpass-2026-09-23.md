## Actual browser playthroughs

Both matches used normal roster selection, mouse orders, keyboard shortcuts and the game's 5× button. No objective, health, inventory, or result edits and no developer `advance()` were used for these playthroughs.

- **Defense:** moved four squads to positions around the village and drew a roughly 70 m engineer trench south of it. The trench completed. Victory at **388.3 simulation seconds**, **21/48 able**; 2,284 shots / 315 hits. This used the survivor-center/input fixes, before the later alert and graphics-recovery patches.
- **Offensive:** sent two squads to A and three toward B; drew the pioneer team's multi-bend route using M and real pointer movement. The engineers finished their route at 112.55 seconds. Victory at **363.3 seconds**, **43/48 able**, 911 shots / 124 hits. Production build `index-CN_twc6t.js`; subsequent navigation changes only affect checked garrison approaches, and later changes were UI/graphics recovery.
- Saved the offensive outcome, reloaded the application, and loaded it in a newer production build. Every serialized field matched the saved record exactly. Starting another mode preserved that save.
- Verified menu pause, Escape close, route cancellation, final-order lock, malformed-save reporting, and a fresh sandbox start. Real user browser storage was never touched.

The final graphics-recovery browser probe used **`index-Ce3k-Vg9.js`**. Time stayed exactly **1.5000000000000007 seconds** through loss, an Escape attempt, and restoration. Resume was disabled during loss. After explicitly resuming, time reached 4.5 seconds and rendering returned to 93 draw calls. The probe captured **zero new console warnings or errors**, including the three-second recovery period. Earlier failed graphics probes and their warning logs remain under `.playwright-cli/`.

Final short production cadence sample: 72-soldier operation, 1440 × 900, Balanced, 5×, after graphics restoration; 10.006 seconds / 1,648 frames, **p95 6.2 ms**, worst 6.4 ms, and 50 simulation seconds advanced. This is a short operation sample, not mature 300/1,000-soldier certification.

## Remaining limits / next scoped checks

Combat difficulty is still a prototype; these two wins are not proof of general balance or enjoyable play. Garrison alerts have headless integration/save tests, not a long browser combat-garrison soak. Mature large-camp performance remains unresolved; the fast 50-person assignment does not certify all congested routes. Complete touch navigation, audio, machine guns and trained AI are not implemented in this pass. A full browser-process or GPU failure that never restores its context still requires saving/reloading; this test covers the recoverable context-loss path.

Next bug-only passes should prioritize long combat-garrison sessions, repeated mode/save/quality changes, interrupted supply emergencies, and crowded/aged camps. Do not restart neural training or expand major systems under the narrowed request.
